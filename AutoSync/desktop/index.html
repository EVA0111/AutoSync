      Sushi needs subtitles timed to the source and matching audio in both versions. Supports ASS / SRT. Styling is preserved; check silent signs and frame-by-frame typesetting in the DRAFT output.

      Subtitle options

      For speech or multilingual matching, install Subtitle AI in Settings. Models download on first use.

      Lossless text formats: ASS/SSA, SRT, VTT, SBV, MicroDVD SUB, MPL2, SAMI and flat TTML/DFXP. PGS SUP works with encode matching or a saved map. VobSub and other binary formats require conversion; OCR is not included. Embedded ASS/SRT/VTT/PGS tracks can be extracted from MKV.

      Audio cannot directly time silent signs. Typesetting without picture evidence, unmapped cues and uncertain cuts are flagged. A DRAFT retains original timing for unmapped cues; review it before muxing. Positions are preserved, not resized for different crops/aspect ratios.
